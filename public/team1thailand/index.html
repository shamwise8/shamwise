Educational focus

Where we put the effort.

Thai & English

We run workshops, content and support in both Thai and English — reaching Thai students and builders as well as the international community based here. Most Avalanche material in Thailand is English only, so the Thai side is where we add the most.

University builder funnel

A rotating workshop tour with a student anchor per campus, handing the cadence to students so the programme continues without us.

Builder support

Getting Thai builders onto Builder Hub, into Team1 mini-grants, and connected to people who can unblock them. Biweekly “Builders Brunch” sessions in Bangkok.

Partnerships & real-world adoption

Thailand has genuine regulated activity in payments, real estate and tokenised assets. We work with partners already doing that work, not only crypto-native ones.

Built by members

Projects coming out of the community.

AcreLabs

A library for Avalanche moments — on-chain badges letting people collect proof of the events they actually attended, with a content layer in progress.

MaLaew (มาแล้ว)

“I've arrived.” Scan a QR at the venue, mint a soulbound attendance badge in seconds — no app install, nothing for organisers to deploy. For most attendees it becomes their first on-chain transaction.

RWA Readiness Radar

An assessment tool for real-world asset projects, scoring readiness against linked evidence and publishing versioned results on Avalanche.
