X login help

Hit a problem while testing? Post it in the team chat with what fixed it, and we'll add it here.
Login fails or loops inside Telegram, LINE, Instagram or Facebook

Those apps open links in their own browser, and X login often breaks there. Open the link in Safari or Chrome instead (tap ··· then "Open in browser").

The X app opens instead of the login page

On iPhone, X links can jump into the X app. Go back, long-press the button and open it in the browser, or log in to x.com in Safari first and try again.

Logged in with the wrong X account

Use Disconnect X in the app, switch accounts on X, then connect again.

"X task access is missing or expired"

Tap "Refresh X access" and log in to X again. Progress is kept.

A post task won't verify

Check the post has the right tags, the hashtag and a photo or video. Still stuck? Open "Use a post link instead" and paste the post's link.

Follow or repost doesn't show as done

X can take a few seconds to update. Wait, then tap Verify again.

Anything else

Send the guest to Pai or Danny. Don't hand out a QR to make up for an app problem.
